DDS tuning-word calculator

Calculate the nearest tuning word for a binary phase accumulator, with the actual output frequency and frequency resolution. Use it when designing a numerically controlled oscillator in FPGA logic.

Calculator · Method · References

Calculator

Phase-accumulator equations

For a binary accumulator updated once per sample, K = roundeven(fout × 2N / fsample). The realized frequency is K × fsample / 2N, and the frequency step is fsample / 2N. The tuning word is rounded using exact decimal ratios and integer arithmetic. Displayed frequencies use floating-point arithmetic.

Example

For a 100 MHz sample clock, a 32-bit accumulator, and a requested 1 MHz output, K is 42,949,673 (0x028F5C29). The realized frequency is approximately 1,000,000.000931 Hz.

Frequency range and implementation

This tool covers nonnegative output frequencies from DC through half the sample rate. At exactly Nyquist, the sampled waveform depends on phase and can degenerate to zero for a sine lookup. Frequencies above half the sample rate alias into the baseband.

Phase width determines the tuning resolution. Amplitude width, phase truncation, dithering, DAC response, and output filtering separately affect spectral quality. For time-multiplexed channels, enter the sample update rate for one channel. AMD DDS Compiler rasterized mode uses a programmable modulus and requires a different calculation.
