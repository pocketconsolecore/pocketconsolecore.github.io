Engineering calculators

Tools for the numbers behind circuits, signals, and digital hardware.

EasyTrig is a collection of free calculators by Perry Newlin. Work through a phasor, check an FPGA timing budget, or generate a testbench. Each engineering tool includes its equations, assumptions, and references.

Calculations run in your browser. All tools are free to use, with light and dark display modes.

Circuits & signals

  • Phasor calculator

    Add, subtract, multiply, and divide in rectangular or polar form.

  • Phasor & sinusoid plotter

    Compare time-domain waveforms with explicit amplitude and phase conventions.

  • Complex number plotter

    Plot vectors on the complex plane and inspect magnitude and argument.

  • Discrete-time signal plotter

    Evaluate sequences, inspect samples, and fit manually entered points.

FPGA engineering

Arithmetic and implementation estimates for digital design, including AMD and Xilinx FPGA workflows. Device-specific assumptions are stated beside the results.

  • Fixed-point converter

    Encode and decode signed or unsigned binary fixed-point values.

  • Clock divider & enable

    Find an integer division ratio, frequency error, and counter width.

  • DDS tuning word

    Calculate a phase increment, achieved frequency, and tuning resolution.

  • FPGA memory sizing

    Estimate block RAM requirements from width, depth, and primitive mode.

  • Setup & hold timing

    Check a single-cycle path with clock skew and uncertainty.

  • UART baud-rate divider

    Choose a sampling divider and quantify baud-rate error.

About this reference

EasyTrig grew from Perry Newlin’s work with circuit analysis and FPGA testbenches. The project connects classroom mathematics with everyday hardware design. Read about Perry, the calculation methodology, and the project license, or explore his engineering portfolio.
