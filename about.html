Calculation methodology

The engineering tools show their formulas, units, conventions, and applicable limits. Known-value and boundary tests cover the mathematical core. Phasor tools distinguish peak and RMS conventions where relevant, fixed-point tools make rounding and overflow explicit, and FPGA resource estimates name the primitive configurations they model.

FPGA sizing and timing calculations are design estimates. Final device utilization and timing depend on the implementation, target part, constraints, placement, routing, and tool configuration. Compare those results with synthesis and static timing reports for your actual design.

Use the reference links on each calculator to inspect the underlying mathematics and device documentation. Worked examples provide reproducible starting points.

Privacy & accessibility

Calculations and document previews run locally in your browser. EasyTrig uses no analytics, advertising, or account system. The selected light or dark mode is stored in your browser. Charts use line patterns and markers, and numerical results remain available as text or tables.
